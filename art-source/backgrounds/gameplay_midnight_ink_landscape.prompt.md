# Gameplay Midnight Ink Landscape Background

The source master `gameplay_midnight_ink_landscape.png` was generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool as the landscape companion to the M7 default gameplay surface.

## Production Prompt

Create a borderless `16:9` landscape companion to the Midnight Ink portrait background. Use a near-black midnight-navy and blue-charcoal Japanese paper surface with subtle handmade fibers, analog grain, scratches, and screen-print distress. Keep a broad uninterrupted low-noise center for the portrait-authored board. Place irregular clusters of manga-ink skulls, five-point stars, mischievous cat faces, sparks, and abstract mahjong-tile doodles along the far left and right edges and lightly in the corners. Use restrained dusty cyan-blue, hot pink, muted violet, and ochre accents.

Keep the upper-left HUD, upper-right Pause, lower-left consumables, right-side tray, and center Board zones readable. Do not include a green cast, border, frame, text, logo, character, tile, panel, control, floral motif, dominant wave pattern, or central focal object.

## Export

- Source master: `1672 x 941` PNG.
- Runtime asset: same dimensions to preserve the authored landscape composition.
- Godot presentation: orientation-selected `NinePatchRect` with the shared `48 px` edge margins.
