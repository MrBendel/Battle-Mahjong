# Gameplay Midnight Ink Background

The source master `gameplay_midnight_ink.png` was generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for the M7 default gameplay theme.

## Production Prompt

Create a darker, non-green replacement for the gameplay background with a quiet center and edge-safe scale-9 structure. Use a near-black blue-charcoal Japanese paper surface with subtle handmade fibers. Concentrate loose manga-ink skulls, five-point stars, mischievous cat faces, tiny spark marks, and occasional abstract mahjong-tile doodles around the far perimeter with irregular spacing and varied scale. Add dry-brush traces with only small oxblood, dusty-cyan, muted-violet, and ochre accents. The surface must extend uninterrupted to every edge with no enclosing frame or rounded border; live HUD and tray components provide the structural framing.

The central play field must remain low-noise so warm ivory ceramic tiles and live HUD components dominate. Do not include text, logos, tiles, controls, characters, green, emerald, teal, casino felt, large red brush arcs, bright graffiti, gradients, or a central focal object.

## Export

- Source master: `941 x 1672` PNG.
- Runtime asset: same dimensions to preserve the authored `48 px` scale-9 margins.
- Godot presentation: `NinePatchRect` with a subtle neutral blue-black wash.
